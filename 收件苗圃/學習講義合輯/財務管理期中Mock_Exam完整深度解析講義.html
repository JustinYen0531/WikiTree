🏛️ 模組 1：公司組織型態與股票市場運作 (Ch01) Q1 ~ Q5

第 1 題 (單選) Ch 1.1 The Types of Firms | Diff: 1
Which of the following organization forms accounts for the most revenue? (下列哪種企業組織型態佔據了整個經濟體中絕大部分的營業收入？)
✅ 正確解答：(C) Corporation

【核心觀念拆解】 雖然在「企業數量（Number of firms）」上，獨資企業（Sole proprietorships）佔了全美 70% 以上，但在「營業收入（Revenue）與經濟影響力」上，大型股份有限公司（Corporations）貢獻了超過 85% 的總營收！因為股份公司具備有限責任與股份可自由轉讓性，能吸納海量資本。

⚠️ 陷阱提醒：看清楚題目問的是「revenue（營業額）」還是「number of businesses（家數）」！家數最多是獨資，營收最高是公司！
第 2 題 (單選) Ch 1.1 The Types of Firms | Diff: 1
Which of the following are subject to double taxation? (下列何種企業型態必須承受「雙重課稅」？)
✅ 正確解答：(A) Corporation

【核心觀念拆解】 雙重課稅（Double Taxation）是標準公司（C-Corp）的最大劣勢：

  • 第一道稅：公司本身的淨利潤要繳納「公司所得稅（Corporate Income Tax）」。
  • 第二道稅：剩餘利潤以股利（Dividends）分派給股東時，股東個人還要再繳一次「個人股利所得稅」。

獨資與一般合夥屬於「轉嫁課稅主體（Pass-through entities）」，利潤直接穿透由個人報稅，沒有雙重課稅問題。

第 3 題 (單選) Ch 1.3 The Stock Market | Diff: 1
How much would you have to pay to purchase 100 XYZ shares on November 18th? (在 11 月 18 日購買 100 股 XYZ 股票需支付多少金額？)
✅ 正確解答：(D) $2,600

【計算與思路】 投資人「購買（Purchase）」股票時，必須向市場支付造市商的賣方開價（Ask Price）。11 月 18 日的 $\text{Ask} = \$26.00$。
總花費 = $100 \text{ 股} \times \$26.00 = \$2,600$。

第 4 題 (單選) Ch 1.3 The Stock Market | Diff: 1
How much would you receive if you sold 200 XYZ shares on November 11th? (在 11 月 11 日賣出 200 股 XYZ 股票能收到多少金額？)
✅ 正確解答：(B) $5,040

【計算與思路】 投資人「賣出（Sell）」股票時，市場造市商用買價（Bid Price）向你收購。11 月 11 日的 $\text{Bid} = \$25.20$。
總收益 = $200 \text{ 股} \times \$25.20 = \$5,040$。

⚠️ 陷阱提醒：千萬別乘錯成 Ask 價格（$200 \times 25.25 = 5,050$），造市商永遠賺取買賣價差（Bid-Ask Spread）！
第 5 題 (單選) Ch 1.3 The Stock Market | Diff: 1
What type of organisation trades on an organized stock exchange? (何種組織型態能在受監管的有組織證券交易所上市交易？)
✅ 正確解答：(D) A public company

【核心觀念拆解】 只有公開發行並上市的股份公司（Public Company）其股份才能在 NYSE、Nasdaq 等有組織的集中交易所供大眾自由撮合買賣；非公開私人公司（Private Company）股份無法公開自由掛牌。

第 6 題 (單選) Ch 3.1 Valuing Decisions | Diff: 2
Assuming you currently have 10,000 Bbls of WTI crude, the added benefits to you if you were to sell the 10,000 Bbls of WTI crude and use the proceeds to purchase and refine ANS crude is closest to: (賣出手上的 10,000 桶 WTI，換買並提煉 ANS 原油，所帶來的額外效益 Added Benefits 最接近：)
✅ 正確解答：(D) $43,308

【解剖精算步驟】

  1. 賣出 10,000 桶 WTI 現金收益 = $10,000 \times \$73.06 = \$730,600$。
  2. 將這筆錢全數購入 ANS 原油桶數 = $\frac{\$730,600}{\$71.75} \approx 10,182.578$ 桶。
  3. 提煉 ANS 產出的汽油總價值 = $10,182.578 \times \$76.00 = \$773,875.96$（若四捨五入取 10,183 桶，則 $10,183 \times \$76 = \$773,908$）。
  4. 對比投入的成本代價（放棄的 WTI 現金價值 $730,600$）：
    額外淨效益 = $\$773,908 - \$730,600 = \$43,308$！
第 7 題 (單選) Ch 3.1 Valuing Decisions | Diff: 2
Another oil refiner is offering to trade you 10,150 Bbls of ANS crude oil for 10,000 Bbls of WTI crude oil. Assuming you currently have 10,000 Bbls of WTI crude, what should you do? (另一家同業提議用 10,150 桶 ANS 來換你手上的 10,000 桶 WTI，你應該怎麼做？)
✅ 正確解答：(A) 拒絕對手提議，自己在公開市場賣掉 WTI 去買 ANS 提煉

【策略邏輯】 由上一題可知，你在市場上賣掉 10,000 桶 WTI（得 $730,600）可以直接買到 10,182.58 桶 的 ANS 原油！
那家同業只願拿 10,150 桶跟你換，等於坑了你 32.58 桶原油！因此絕不接受交換，自己在市場買賣才是最優決策。

第 8 題 (單選) Ch 3.2 Interest Rates and Time Value | Diff: 1
A project that you are considering today is expected to provide benefits worth $168,000 in one year. If the risk-free rate of interest ($r_f$) is 4.5%, then the value of the benefits of this project today are closest to: (一年後產生 $168,000 現金流，無風險利率 4.5%，今天現值為多少？)
✅ 正確解答：(B) $160,766

【公式計算】 $PV = \frac{FV}{1 + r} = \frac{\$168,000}{1 + 0.045} = \frac{168,000}{1.045} \approx \$160,765.55 \approx \$160,766$。

第 9 題 (單選) Ch 3.2 Interest Rates and Time Value | Diff: 1
If the risk-free rate of interest ($r_f$) is 6%, then you should be indifferent between receiving $250 in one year or: (若無風險利率 6%，一年後拿 $250 等同於今天拿多少？)
✅ 正確解答：(A) $235.85 today

【公式計算】 $PV = \frac{\$250}{1 + 0.06} = \frac{250}{1.06} \approx \$235.849 \approx \$235.85$。
在無套利金融市場中，今天的 $235.85 與一年後的 $250 效用完全無差異（Indifferent）。

第 10 題 (單選) Ch 3.4 Arbitrage and the Law of One Price | Diff: 2
Which of the following statements regarding arbitrage is the most correct? (關於「套利 Arbitrage」的陳述，何者最為正確？)
✅ 正確解答：(D) 在不同市場買賣等價商品以賺取價差之行為

【觀念辨析】 標準套利定義包含兩大特徵：零成本且零風險賺取確定利潤。但教科書中對套利操作的嚴格行為定義是 (D)：「在不同市場中同時買進被低估、賣出被高估的等價商品以賺取價差」。(A) 缺了零投資條件，(B) 缺了零風險條件，(C) 無套利市場稱為正常市場而非套利市場。

第 11 題 (單選) Ch 3.4 Arbitrage and the Law of One Price | Diff: 2
Which of the following statements regarding the Law of One Price is INCORRECT? (關於「單一價格法則」的敘述，下列何者「錯誤」？)
✅ 正確解答：(D) 錯誤，單一價格法則必須依賴「套利力量」才能維持

【深度剖析】 單一價格法則（Law of One Price）能夠成立的**物理機制**就是「套利者的存在」！一旦有價差，套利者立刻買低賣高將價格拉平；如果一個市場完全禁止套利（例如封閉邊境、禁止轉賣），價差就會永久存在，單一價格法則必然失效！因此 (D) 宣稱「即使無法套利的市場法則依然成立」是徹底錯誤的。

第 12 題 (單選) Ch 3.5 No-Arbitrage and Security Prices | Diff: 1
Suppose that Bondi Inc. is a holding company that owns both Pizza Hut and Kentucky Fried Chicken Franchised Restaurants. If the value of Bondi is $130 million, and the Pizza Hut Franchises are worth $70 million, then what is the value of the Kentucky Fried Chicken Franchises? (控股公司價值 1.3 億，其中必勝客值 7,000 萬，肯德基值多少？)
✅ 正確解答：(A) $60 million

【價值可加性 Value Additivity】 資產組合的總價值等於其各組成部分價值之和：
$V_{\text{Bondi}} = V_{\text{Pizza Hut}} + V_{\text{KFC}} \implies 130 = 70 + V_{\text{KFC}} \implies V_{\text{KFC}} = \$60\text{ million}$。

第 13 題 (單選) Ch 3.5 No-Arbitrage and Security Prices | Diff: 3
An American Depository Receipt (ADR) is a security issued by a U.S. bank and traded on a U.S. stock exchange that represents a specific number of shares of a foreign stock. Siemens AG has an ADR that trades on the NYSE and is equivalent to one share of Seimens AG trading on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in Germany. If Seimens trades for $95.19 on the NYSE and for €64.10 on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange, then under the law of one price, the current exchange rate is closest to:

$\$95.19 = \text{€}64.10$。
每 1 美元等值多少歐元：$\frac{\text{€}64.10}{\$95.19} \approx \text{€}0.67339 / \$ \approx \text{€}0.6734 / \$$！
（若求每歐元等值多少美元，則為 $95.19 / 64.10 \approx \$1.485 / \text{€}$，注意選項 (C) 單位寫成 €/$\$$ 故錯誤）。

第 14 題 (單選) Ch 3.5 No-Arbitrage and Security Prices | Diff: 2
An ETF represents a portfolio: 2 shares of Apple ($200.23/sh), 1 share of Google ($570.51/sh), and 10 shares of Microsoft ($29.61/sh). In a normal market, the price per share of this ETF is closest to:
✅ 正確解答：(D) $1,267

第 15 題 (單選) Ch 3.5 No-Arbitrage and Security Prices | Diff: 1
Suppose that a security with a risk-free cash flow of $1,000 in one year trades for $930 today. If there are no arbitrage opportunities, then the current risk-free rate is closest to: (一年後確定支付 $1,000 的無風險證券今天賣 $930，無風險利率為？)

⏳ 模組 3：時間軸、貨幣時間價值與年金實戰 (Ch04) Q16 ~ Q28

第 16 題 (單選) Ch 4.1 The Timeline | Diff: 1
Which of the following statements regarding timelines is FALSE? (關於「時間軸 Timeline」的敘述，何者「錯誤」？)
✅ 正確解答：(C) 錯誤，任何現金流皆可在時間軸上精確標示

【觀念說明】 時間軸是財務學最通用的工具。無論是等額、非等額、遞延或永續，**任何現金流量（Any stream of cash flows）** 都可以在時間軸上清楚標註發生時間點與金額。因此 (C)「不是每種現金流都能畫」是錯誤的。

第 19 題 (單選) Ch 4.4 Calculating Net Present Value | Diff: 2
Wyatt oil considers drilling an oil well costing $1,000,000. It produces $100,000 worth of oil in year 1, declining by 2% per year forever. If interest rate is 8%, what is NPV? (油井成本 100 萬，第一年產出 10 萬，之後每年永久衰退 2%，利率 8%，淨現值 NPV 為？)
✅ 正確解答：(B) $0

【成長型永續年金 Growing Perpetuity】

$$PV = \frac{C_1}{r - g}$$

這裡「每年遞減 2%」，所以成長率是負數：$g = -2\% = -0.02$！
$PV = \frac{100,000}{0.08 - (-0.02)} = \frac{100,000}{0.10} = \$1,000,000$。
因此，$\text{NPV} = -1,000,000 + 1,000,000 = \$0$！

⚠️ 陷阱提醒：負負得正！分母是 $0.08 - (-0.02) = 0.10$，千萬別算成 $0.08 - 0.02 = 0.06$！
🎓 題組背景 (Q20 & Q21)： 新生兒大學學費規劃

剛迎來新生兒，目前大學一年學費為 $12,500。學費歷史年增率（通膨）為 4%。孩子 18 歲入學。

第 20 題 (單選) Ch 4.5 Perpetuities and Annuities | Diff: 2
Assuming that costs continue to increase at 4% per year, tuition and other costs for ONE YEAR for this student in 18 years will be closest to: (18 年後第一年的大學學費是多少？)
✅ 正確解答：(D) $25,323

【複利終值計算】 $FV = PV \times (1 + g)^N = \$12,500 \times (1.04)^{18} \approx 12,500 \times 2.025817 = \$25,322.71 \approx \$25,323$。

第 21 題 (單選) Ch 4.5 Perpetuities and Annuities | Diff: 3
Assuming tuition increases at 4% per year and savings account pays 7% interest, what amount must be available at age 18 to pay for ALL FOUR YEARS of undergraduate education? (在 18 歲時，帳戶必須準備多少資金才能完全付清四年的成長型學費？)
✅ 正確解答：(A) $97,110

【成長型期初年金現值 Annuity Due】
18 歲當下立即要繳第一筆學費 $C_1 = \$25,322.71$，共 4 期，每年增長 $g = 4\%$，折現率 $r = 7\%$：
$$PV = C_1 \times \frac{1}{r - g} \left[1 - \left(\frac{1 + g}{1 + r}\right)^N\right] \times (1 + r)$$ $$PV = 25,322.71 \times \frac{1}{0.07 - 0.04} \left[1 - \left(\frac{1.04}{1.07}\right)^4\right] \times 1.07$$ $$= 25,322.71 \times 33.3333 \times [1 - 0.89205] \times 1.07 \approx \$97,110.01$$

第 22 題 (單選) Ch 4.5 Perpetuities and Annuities | Diff: 1
The British government has a consol bond paying £100 in interest each year forever. If interest rate is 5% and first payment is one year from now, the value of the consol bond is: (英國政府統一公債每年發放 £100 永續利息，利率 5%，現值為？)
✅ 正確解答：(D) £2,000

【標準永續年金 Perpetuity】 $PV = \frac{C}{r} = \frac{£100}{0.05} = £2,000$。

第 23 題 (單選) Ch 4.5 Perpetuities and Annuities | Diff: 1
Consider a growing perpetuity that pays $100 in one year, growing by 6% each year forever. If the interest rate is 11%, the value of this perpetuity is: (成長型永續年金第一年 $100，成長率 6%，折現率 11%，價值為？)
✅ 正確解答：(C) $2,000

【公式計算】 $PV = \frac{C}{r - g} = \frac{100}{0.11 - 0.06} = \frac{100}{0.05} = \$2,000$。

第 24 題 (單選) Ch 4.5 Perpetuities and Annuities | Diff: 2
Son starts kindergarten in private school. Current tuition is $12,000/yr, payable AT THE START of school year. Tuition increases 6% per year for next 13 years. Interest rate is 7%. What is PV?
✅ 正確解答：(C) $155,800（原試卷官方給分選項為 C，註明出題者選項有瑕疵）

【考卷原題瑕疵透視】

  • 普通期末年金算出的現值為：$\$12,000 \times \frac{1}{0.07 - 0.06} [1 - (\frac{1.06}{1.07})^{13}] = \$137,893$（對應選項 B）。
  • 但題目明確說明「payable at the start of school year (期初年金 Annuity Due)」，必須再乘上 $(1 + r)$：

📜 官方期中必備公式大全 (Midterm Formula Sheet)

1. 單筆現金流與複利

  • $PV = \frac{FV}{(1 + i)^t}$
  • $FV = PV \times (1 + i)^t$
  • 連續複利：$FV = PV \times e^{r t}$
  • $EAR = (1 + \frac{APR}{m})^m - 1$

2. 年金與永續年金

  • 普通年金現值：$PV = \frac{C}{r} [1 - (1 + r)^{-N}]$
  • 永續年金現值：$PV = \frac{C}{r}$
  • 成長型永續年金：$PV = \frac{C_1}{r - g}$
  • 期初年金：$PV_{\text{due}} = PV_{\text{ord}} \times (1 + r)$

3. 債券定價與收益率

  • $P = \sum_{t=1}^N \frac{C}{(1+y)^t} + \frac{FV}{(1+y)^N}$
  • 零息債券殖利率：$YTM_n = (\frac{FV}{P})^{1/n} - 1$
  • 當期收益率：$Current Yield = \frac{C}{P_0}$
  • 信用利差：$Credit Spread = YTM_{\text{corp}} - YTM_{\text{treasury}}$

4. 投資組合與 WACC (後續進階)

  • CAPM: $k_i = k_{rf} + [k_m - k_{rf}] \beta_i$
  • WACC $= w_e k_e + w_d k_d(1 - T) + w_p k_p$
  • 費雪公式：$1 + r_r = \frac{1 + r}{1 + i}$
  • 稅後利率：$r_{\text{after-tax}} = r(1 - \tau)$
